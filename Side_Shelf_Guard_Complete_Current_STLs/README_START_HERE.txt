RECOMMENDED PRINT STARTER SETTINGS
  PETG, 0.20 mm layers, 4 walls on brackets and bridges, no supports as
  starting point. Check the 45-degree panel bed placement in slicer.
  PETG clips can break if spread too far; stop if force is excessive.

IMPORTANT
  The bridge opening widths are nominal CAD dimensions. Actual snap behavior
  and fit must be checked on your printed parts. Existing snap flexibility and
  exact 11.5-inch fence length are not physically validated as an assembly.
  End pegs at fence edges limit motion only when there are brackets near the
  ends. A single centrally placed bridge may allow the fence to slide
  substantially before its stop contacts a bracket.
